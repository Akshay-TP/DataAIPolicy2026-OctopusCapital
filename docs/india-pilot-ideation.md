# India pilot scope

FieldLedger extends field support without a live BR.NET integration. It uses optional organisation member references, standalone worker profiles, voluntarily supplied information and explicit unknowns. It does not produce a credit score or make lending decisions.

## Main pathways

- Work and advance debt in India, including reported concerns requiring specialist bonded-labour support.
- Indian overseas workers and their families: recruitment debt, salary receipts, deductions and remittances.

## Smaller modules

- Second chance: prospective-member meetings, voluntary contributions, missing information and documented human reconsideration.
- Loan-use and livelihood checks: intended purchase, actual reported use, functioning status, separate household explanations and officer observations, and follow-up.

## Implemented workflows

Pay expectations and receipts, reported debt and repayments, deductions with explicit debt links, remittance receipt confirmation, explainable late/short-pay flags and assigned support cases are connected to encrypted persistence and offline capture. Source, date, consent and unknown values remain visible. A linked wage deduction is counted once as a debt repayment. Remittances are separate transfers, not additional wages.

The application does not send referrals, determine legal status, verify employer allegations or automatically approve or change loans. Warning thresholds are adjustable and unvalidated. A short livelihood check is available; a 30-second field-use target has not been validated.
